Subject: Call for Papers – PLOS ONE

PLOS ONE (eISSN 1932-6203) is a peer-reviewed, open access journal published by the
Public Library of Science (PLOS). It is indexed in Scopus, Web of Science and DOAJ.

We welcome submissions of original research across the natural sciences, medical
research, engineering and the social sciences. All manuscripts undergo full peer review.
See the journal website for current article processing charges.

Journal website: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/
Questions about submissions: plosone@plos.org

Submissions for this collection close on 31 January 2027.
