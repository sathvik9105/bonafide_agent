Subject: Call for Papers – Nordic Workshop on Sustainable Computing Systems (NWSCS 2027)

Dear colleague,

We are pleased to invite submissions to the Nordic Workshop on Sustainable Computing Systems
(NWSCS 2027), a small single-track workshop to be held 15-16 March 2027 in Espoo, Finland.

The workshop welcomes short papers (4-6 pages) on energy-aware software, green data centres
and carbon-aware scheduling. Every submission receives at least three peer reviews, and
authors are notified of decisions within eight weeks of the submission deadline. Accepted
papers will appear in the workshop's online proceedings.

Invited speakers:
- Dr. Clara Frescati, Stockholm University (Keynote) – c.frescati@su.example
- Prof. Livia Albano, KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Invited talk) – livia.albano@kth.example

Important dates:
- Paper submission: 30 November 2026
- Notification of acceptance: 25 January 2027

Registration: EUR 180 (students EUR 90).
Website: https://www.nwscs-workshop.org
Contact: program@nwscs-workshop.org
